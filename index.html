Cold Storage 9

The power failed at 2 a.m. Night clerks walk the aisles now. Something smaller crawls under the racks and hears you when you run. Chapter 1 is a cold storage warehouse. Chapter 2 is a night grocery store. Chapter 3 is a parking garage. Chapter 4 is a hospital wing. Each one is bigger than the last. Find three fuses per chapter, bring the gate online, and get out.

Mouse: click the screen, then move the mouse. If the view does not move, hold the left button and drag. Keys: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, Ctrl crouch, E hide in a locker, P pause, 1 to 4 use slots. Controller: sticks, A jump, RT run, LB crouch, B hide, X flashlight.

Codes: LOWPOWER, more FPS and less detail. SHARP, 1.25x detail with shadows. REALISTIC, 1.5x detail with sharper shadows.
